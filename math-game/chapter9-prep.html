Probability — Chance & Events / 機率

🎲 9.1 — Probability of Simple Events

Probability measures how likely an event is. Every probability lives in [0, 1]: 0 = impossible, 1 = certain. For equally likely outcomes:

Key Formulas / 關鍵公式

Probability 機率

P(E) = favorable ÷ total
How many good ÷ how many total

Range 範圍

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1
0 = never, 1 = always

Complement 互補事件

P(not E) = 1 − P(E)
Everything NOT in E

Sample Space 樣本空間

S = {all outcomes}
Die: S = {1,2,3,4,5,6}

Sum = 1 總和為 1

Σ P(outcomes) = 1
All possibilities together

Percent / Decimal 形式

1/4 = 0.25 = 25%
Three ways to write the same P
🎲
Example 1: Rolling a Fair Die / 擲公平骰子
SIMPLE EVENT / 簡單事件
1
Sample space / 樣本空間
S = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}. Six equally likely outcomes.
2
P(rolling a 4) / 擲出 4 的機率
P(4) = 1 ÷ 6 = 1/6
3
P(rolling an even number) / 擲出偶數
Favorable = {2, 4, 6}. That’s 3 outcomes.
P(even) = 3 ÷ 6 = 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%
4
P(NOT a 4) — complement / 不是 4
P(not 4) = 1 − 1/6 = 5/6
💡 Pro Tip
When computing a probability, always write down the sample space first. Favorable over total is only that simple when outcomes are equally likely. / 事件等機率時才能這樣算。
🔴
Example 2: Marble Draw / 抽彈珠
SINGLE DRAW / 單次抽取
1
Bag contents / 袋內
5 red, 3 blue, 2 green marbles. Total = 10.
2
P(red) / 抽到紅
P(red) = 5/10 = 1/2
3
P(blue OR green) / 藍或綠
Favorable = 3 + 2 = 5.
P(B or G) = 5/10 = 1/2
4
Check with complement / 用互補驗算
P(not red) = 1 − 1/2 = 1/2 ✓
Same as P(blue or green) — nice consistency.
💡 Pro Tip
When an event means “anything BUT X”, computing 1 − P(X) is usually faster than adding up the rest. / ‘不是 X’ 用互補最快。
🌀
Example 3: Uneven Spinner / 不均等轉盤
NON-UNIFORM / 非均等
1
Spinner sectors / 扇形比例
Red covers 180°, Blue 90°, Yellow 60°, Green 30°. Total = 360°.
2
Probability = fraction of circle / 機率 = 圓的比例
P(red) = 180/360 = 1/2
P(blue) = 90/360 = 1/4
P(yellow) = 60/360 = 1/6
P(green) = 30/360 = 1/12
3
Check sum = 1 / 總和驗算
1/2 + 1/4 + 1/6 + 1/12 = 6/12 + 3/12 + 2/12 + 1/12 = 12/12 = 1 ✓
💡 Pro Tip
When outcomes are not equally likely, probabilities must come from measurable shares (angles, areas, counts). Then verify: they must sum to 1. / 非等機率時要用面積/比例，總和必為 1。

🔬 9.2 — Theoretical vs Experimental

Theoretical probability comes from the math of a fair setup. Experimental probability comes from what actually happened in trials. More trials → experimental gets closer to theoretical.

Key Ideas / 關鍵概念

Theoretical 理論

P = favorable ÷ total
From the structure (fair coin = 1/2)

Experimental 實驗

P = successes ÷ trials
From data collected

Relative Frequency 相對頻率

count ÷ n
Another name for experimental P

Law of Large Numbers 大數法則

n → ∞ ⇒ exp → theo
Many trials → they agree

Expected Count 期望次數

n × P(E)
How many hits in n trials

Fair vs Biased 公平與偏差

exp ≠ theo ⇒ suspect
Big gap over many trials
🪙
Example 1: Coin Flips — Theo vs Exp / 擲硬幣
COMPARISON / 比較
1
Theoretical / 理論
A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes.
P(H) = 1/2 = 0.5
2
Experimental (20 flips) / 實驗 (20 次)
Observed: 12 heads, 8 tails.
Pexp(H) = 12/20 = 0.6
3
Experimental (200 flips) / 實驗 (200 次)
Observed: 96 heads, 104 tails.
Pexp(H) = 96/200 = 0.48
Closer to 0.5 — big samples trend toward theoretical.
4
Expected count / 期望次數
In 300 flips, expect 300 × 0.5 = 150 heads
💡 Law of Large Numbers
A short run can be wildly off theoretical. The Law of Large Numbers says the gap shrinks as trials grow. / 次數多，實驗越接近理論。
⚖️
Example 2: Biased Spinner? / 轉盤有偏？
INFERENCE / 推論
1
Looks like a fair 4-color spinner / 看似公平
Theoretical: P(each) = 1/4 = 0.25. After 200 spins, expect each color 50 times.
2
Observed / 觀察
Red 88, Blue 42, Green 38, Yellow 32. Total = 200.
3
Experimental probabilities / 實驗機率
P(red) = 88/200 = 0.44
P(blue) = 0.21, P(green) = 0.19, P(yellow) = 0.16
4
Inference / 推論
Red’s experimental P (0.44) is way above theoretical 0.25 — with 200 trials, this gap suggests the spinner is biased toward red.
💡 Pro Tip
A small gap across many trials = likely random noise. A big gap across many trials = real bias. More trials sharpen the signal. / 多次實驗有差距才算偏。
🏁
Example 3: Expected Wins / 預期贏的次數
WORD PROBLEM / 文字題
1
Game P(win) / 遊戲贏的機率
In a carnival game, the theoretical probability of winning is 1/5.
2
Kayla plays 60 times / 玩 60 次
Expected wins = 60 × 1/5 = 12
3
She actually wins 18 times / 實際贏 18 次
Pexp(win) = 18/60 = 0.30
That’s higher than 0.20 — lucky streak or the game is easier than advertised?
💡 Pro Tip
Expected count = n × P. It’s an average, not a guarantee. 12 expected wins could show up as 8 or 16 in a real session. / 期望次數是平均，不一定剛好。

🌳 9.3 — Compound Events

Two events at once. Independent: one doesn’t affect the other (two dice, coin + spinner). Dependent: first event changes the setup for the second (draw 2 marbles without replacement).

Key Rules / 關鍵規則

Independent AND 獨立 &

P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
Multiply when both must happen

Dependent AND 相依 &

P(A) × P(B | A)
2nd probability uses new total

Mutually Exclusive OR 互斥 或

P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)
Can’t both happen (heads OR tails)

General OR 一般或

P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)
Subtract the overlap once

Tree Diagram 樹狀圖

branch = ×, leaves = +
Multiply along path, add paths

With/Without Replacement 放回與否

replaced → independent
Not replaced → dependent
🎲🎲
Example 1: Two Dice (Independent) / 兩顆骰子
INDEPENDENT AND / 獨立 且
1
Two dice, each independent / 獨立
P(6 on die 1) = 1/6, P(6 on die 2) = 1/6.
2
P(both are 6) / 兩個都 6
P(6 and 6) = 1/6 × 1/6 = 1/36
3
P(sum = 7) / 加總為 7
Sample space has 36 pairs; sum-7 pairs: (1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1) = 6.
P(sum = 7) = 6/36 = 1/6
4
P(at least one 6) / 至少一個 6
Complement is easier! P(no 6 on either) = 5/6 × 5/6 = 25/36.
P(≥ 1 six) = 1 − 25/36 = 11/36
💡 Pro Tip
For “at least one” questions, computing the complement (“none of them”) is almost always easier. / 至少一個 → 用互補。
🔴
Example 2: Marbles, No Replacement / 不放回
DEPENDENT AND / 相依 且
1
Setup / 設定
Bag: 4 red, 6 blue (total 10). Draw 2 marbles WITHOUT replacement.
2
P(first red) / 第一顆紅
P(R1) = 4/10 = 2/5
3
P(second red | first red) / 條件機率
After taking 1 red, bag has 3 red & 6 blue = 9 total.
P(R2 | R1) = 3/9 = 1/3
4
P(both red) / 兩顆都紅
P(R & R) = 2/5 × 1/3 = 2/15
💡 Pro Tip
“Without replacement” = dependent. The 2nd draw’s denominator drops by 1, and the numerator drops by 1 if the first event succeeded. / 不放回就變相依。
🌳
Example 3: Tree Diagram / 樹狀圖
VISUAL / 圖解
1
Two-stage experiment / 兩階段
Flip a coin, then spin a 3-color spinner (Red, Blue, Green — equally likely).
2
Total outcomes / 總結果
2 coin × 3 spinner = 6 equally likely paths: HR, HB, HG, TR, TB, TG.
3
P(Heads AND Red) / 頭且紅
P(H and R) = 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6
4
P(Heads OR Red) / 頭或紅
P(H or R) = 1/2 + 1/3 − 1/6 = 3/6 + 2/6 − 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3
Subtract the overlap (HR) because it’s counted in both halves.
💡 Pro Tip
Along a tree path, multiply. Across different leaves, add. The tree makes compound events impossible to mess up. / 路上乘，葉間加。
