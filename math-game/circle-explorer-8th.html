Radius 半徑

The distance from the center to the edge — like paddling from the middle of a tide pool to the rim. Every radius is the same length.
從圓心到边缘的距离——就像從潮池中心划到边缘。同一个圓的每条半徑都一样长。

r = d ÷ 2
🌊

Diameter 直徑

A line straight across through the center — like swimming from one side of a circular reef to the other. Always twice the radius.
穿过圓心的一条直线——就像從珊瑚礁的一边游到另一边。永远是半徑的兩倍。

d = 2r
🐚

Circumference 周長

The total distance around the circle — like surfing the entire perimeter of a wave ring. Uses the magical number π ≈ 3.14159.
圓的一周的總距離——就像沿著浪花圈衝浪一整圈。用到神奇的數字 π ≈ 3.14159。

C = 2πr = πd
☀️

Area 面積

The space inside — like the sandy floor of a circular lagoon. Double the radius and the area quadruples!
圓內部的空间——就像圓形潟湖的沙底。半徑变为兩倍，面積就变为四倍！

A = πr²
📐

Central Angle 圓心角

The angle formed at the center between two radii — like the opening of a fan or the slice angle of a pizza. Measured in degrees (0°–360°).
兩條半徑在圓心形成的角——就像扇子的開口角度或披薩的切角。用度數衡量（0°–360°）。

θ = (arc length ÷ circumference) × 360°
🌈

Arc Length 弧長

The distance along the curved edge between two points — like the length of shoreline between two piers. It's a fraction of the full circumference based on the angle.
圓弧上兩點之間的曲線距離——就像兩個碼頭之間的海岸線長度。根據角度，它是整個周長的一部分。

s = (θ ÷ 360) × 2πr
🏖️

Sector Area 扇形面積

The area of the "pizza slice" — the region enclosed by two radii and the arc. It's a fraction of the full circle's area based on the angle.
「披薩片」的面積——由兩條半徑和弧圍成的區域。根據角度，它是整個圓面積的一部分。

Asector = (θ ÷ 360) × πr²
🚧

Sector Perimeter 扇形周界

The total distance around the sector — two straight edges (radii) plus the curved arc. Like fencing around a pie slice.
扇形的總邊長——兩條直邊（半徑）加上弧長。就像在一塊派的四周圍上柵欄。

Psector = 2r + arc length
🪀

Chord 弦

A straight line connecting two points on the circle — like a rope stretched across a bay. The diameter is the longest possible chord.
連接圓上兩點的直線——就像一條繩子橫跨海灣。直徑是最長的弦。

chord = 2r × sin(θ ÷ 2)

Advanced Circle Applications / 圓的進階應用

Learn to solve irregular shapes using circle concepts!

🎯 Pattern Recognition Guide / 模式識別指南

🥕 Subtraction Method

When a shape has pieces removed (like quarter-circles from a square), calculate the whole shape first, then subtract the removed parts.
當形狀有缺失部分時，先算整體再減去缺失。

🧲 Addition Method

When a shape is made of multiple parts (like combined semicircles), calculate each piece separately, then add them together.
當形狀由多個部分組成時，分別計算再加總。

🔄 Fraction Trick

For sectors, always find the fraction of the circle first: θ/360°. Then multiply by full circle area or circumference.
扇形先找圓的比例：θ/360°，再乘整圓面積或周長。

🚦 Perimeter vs Area

Perimeter is the sum of all boundary lines. Area is the space inside. For sectors, perimeter = 2r + arc!
周界是所有邊界線的總和。面積是內部空間。扇形周界 = 2r + 弧長！

Problem 1: Sector with Central Angle / 圓心角扇形
SUBTRACTION TYPE / 減法類型
1
Identify given information / 確認已知條件
Radius r = 90 cm, Central angle θ = 120°
2
Find the fraction of the circle / 找出圓的比例
Fraction = θ/360° = 120°/360° = 1/3
3
Calculate Arc Length / 計算弧長
Arc = (1/3) × 2πr = (1/3) × 2π × 90 = 60π ≈ 188.5 cm
4
Calculate Sector Perimeter / 計算扇形周界
Perimeter = 2r + Arc = 180 + 60π = 60π + 180 ≈ 368.5 cm
5
Calculate Sector Area / 計算扇形面積
Area = (1/3) × πr² = (1/3) × π × 8100 = 2700π ≈ 8,478 cm²
💡 Pro Tip / 專業提示
For sector perimeter, remember to include BOTH radii! Many students forget the straight edges. / 計算扇形周界時，記得包含兩條半徑！很多學生會忘記直邊。

Problem 2: Square minus Quarter-Circles / 正方形減去四分之一圓
SUBTRACTION METHOD / 減去法
1
Visualize the shape / 想像形狀
Square with side 30 cm. Four quarter-circles at corners (r=15). The center star is shaded.
2
Key insight / 關鍵洞察
4 quarter-circles = 1 complete circle! Each has r = 15 (half the side).
4 × (1/4) = 1 full circle
3
Calculate Shaded Area / 計算陰影面積
Area = Square - Circle = 30² - π(15)² = 900 - 225π = 900 - 225π ≈ 193.5 cm²
4
Calculate Perimeter / 計算周界
The perimeter is just the 4 arcs (they form one circle's circumference)!
Perimeter = 4 × (1/4 × 2πr) = 2π(15) = 30π ≈ 94.2 cm
💡 Pattern Alert / 模式警報
When you see 4 quarter-circles at corners of a square, they always make exactly 1 full circle! / 當你看到正方形的四個角落有四個四分之一圓時，它們總是組成恰好一個完整的圓！
◖
Problem 3: Nested Curves / 嵌套曲線
SUBTRACTION WITH DIFFERENT RADII / 不同半徑的減法
1
Understand the structure / 理解結構
Square 20×20. Large quarter-circle (r=20). Small semicircle on edge (d=20, so r=10).
2
Calculate Quarter-Circle Area / 計算四分之一圓面積
A₁ = (1/4) × π × 20² = (1/4) × 400π = 100π cm²
3
Calculate Semicircle Area / 計算半圓面積
A₂ = (1/2) × π × 10² = (1/2) × 100π = 50π cm²
4
Find Shaded Area / 求陰影面積
Area = A₁ - A₂ = 100π - 50π = 50π ≈ 157 cm²
5
Calculate Perimeter / 計算周界
Quarter-circle arc + Semicircle arc (the straight edges cancel out!)
Arc₁ = (1/4) × 2π × 20 = 10π
Arc₂ = (1/2) × 2π × 10 = 10π
Perimeter = 10π + 10π = 20π ≈ 62.8 cm
💡 Edge Cancel Trick / 邊緣抵消技巧
Notice the straight edge of the semicircle sits ON the straight edge of the quarter-circle — they don't count toward the perimeter! / 注意半圓的直邊坐落在四分之一圓的直邊上——它們不算在周界內！

Problem 4: Composite Semicircles / 複合半圓
ADDITION METHOD / 加法方法
1
Analyze the composite / 分析組合結構
Large semicircle (d=24, r=12) facing down. Two small semicircles (d=16, r=8 and d=8, r=4) facing up.
2
Check: Do diameters add up? / 檢查：直徑相加？
16 + 8 = 24 ✓ The small diameters equal the large one. This confirms they fit perfectly!
3
Calculate Perimeter (all arcs) / 計算周界（所有弧）
Large arc = π × 12 = 12π
Left arc = π × 8 = 8π
Right arc = π × 4 = 4π
Total = 12π + 8π + 4π = 24π ≈ 75.4 cm
4
Calculate Total Area / 計算總面積
Area = ½π(12²) + ½π(8²) + ½π(4²)
= ½π(144 + 64 + 16)
= ½π × 224 = 112π ≈ 352 cm²
💡 The Magic Pattern / 神奇模式
When semicircles sit on a straight line with touching diameters, the perimeter is simply π × (sum of radii). Here: π(12+8+4) = 24π! / 當半圓的直徑在直線上相連時，周界就是 π × (半徑總和)。

Example 1: True/False — Same Angle, Same Arc? / 同角度同弧長？
CONCEPTUAL / 概念題
1
Read the statement / 閱讀題目
“If the central angle of Sector A is twice that of Sector B, the arc length of A must also be twice that of B.”
2
Recall the formula / 回憶公式
Arc = (θ/360) × 2πr
Arc depends on BOTH θ and r!
3
Test with a counterexample / 用反例驗證
Sector A: θ=120°, r=3 → Arc = 2π
Sector B: θ=60°, r=10 → Arc = 10π/3
Arc A < Arc B despite θA = 2×θB!
💡 Answer / 答案
FALSE 錯 — Only true when both sectors have the same radius. Always check! / 只有半徑相同時才成立。
⭕
Example 2: Circumference > 12? / 周長大於12？
TRUE/FALSE CALCULATION / 是非計算
1
Identify given info / 確認已知
Circle with radius r = 2 m. Question: Is C > 12?
2
Calculate / 計算
C = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 2 = 12.56 m
3
Compare / 比較
12.56 > 12 ✓
💡 Answer / 答案
TRUE 對 — When in doubt, just calculate! C = 2πr. / 有疑問就直接算！

Example 3: Quarter-Circle Sector Perimeter / 四分之一圓周長
SECTOR PERIMETER / 扇形周長
1
Identify / 確認
Quarter circle: θ = 90°, r = 70 cm, π = 22/7
2
Find arc length / 求弧長
Arc = (90/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 70 = (1/4) × 440 = 110 cm
3
Add all edges / 加總所有邊
Perimeter = r + r + arc = 70 + 70 + 110 = 250 cm
💡 Pro Tip / 專業提示
Sector perimeter = 2r + arc. Don’t forget the two straight edges! / 扇形周長 = 2r + 弧長，別忘了直線邊！

Example 4: Wheel Rolling Distance / 車輪滾動距離
CIRCUMFERENCE APPLICATION / 周長應用
1
Identify / 確認
Wheel radius = 35 cm, rolls 25 times. π = 22/7
2
One rotation = circumference / 一圈 = 周長
C = 2 × (22/7) × 35 = 2 × 22 × 5 = 220 cm
3
Multiply / 乘以圈數
Distance = 25 × 220 = 5500 cm = 55 m
💡 Pro Tip / 專業提示
Each full rotation = one circumference of distance. / 每滾一圈 = 一個周長的距離。

Example 5: Sector Park Walk / 扇形公園步行
SECTOR BOUNDARY / 扇形邊界
1
Identify / 確認
Sector park: r = 6 m, θ = 120°, π = 22/7
2
Find arc length / 求弧長
Arc = (120/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 6 = (1/3) × 264/7 = 88/7 ≈ 12.57 m
3
Total boundary / 總邊界
Walk = 2r + arc = 12 + 88/7 = 172/7 ≈ 24.57 m
💡 Pro Tip / 專業提示
Walking the full boundary = two radii + the arc. Keep fractions with 22/7! / 走完邊界 = 兩段半徑 + 弧長。
