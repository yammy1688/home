🌊 Unrolling the Wave / 展開海浪

Watch the circumference unroll like a wave stretching along the shore — it equals exactly π × diameter
看周長像海浪一样展开——它恰好等于 π × 直徑

🏄

Radius 半徑

The distance from the center to the edge — like paddling from the middle of a tide pool to the rim. Every radius is the same length.
從圓心到边缘的距离——就像從潮池中心划到边缘。同一个圓的每条半徑都一样长。

r = d ÷ 2
🌊

Diameter 直徑

A line straight across through the center — like swimming from one side of a circular reef to the other. Always twice the radius.
穿过圓心的一条直线——就像從珊瑚礁的一边游到另一边。永远是半徑的兩倍。

d = 2r
🐚

Circumference 周長

The total distance around the circle — like surfing the entire perimeter of a wave ring. Uses the magical number π ≈ 3.14159.
圓的一周的總距離——就像沿著浪花圈衝浪一整圈。用到神奇的數字 π ≈ 3.14159。

C = 2πr = πd
☀️

Area 面積

The space inside — like the sandy floor of a circular lagoon. Double the radius and the area quadruples!
圓內部的空间——就像圓形潟湖的沙底。半徑变为兩倍，面積就变为四倍！

A = πr²
📐

Central Angle 圓心角

The angle formed at the center between two radii — like the opening of a fan or the slice angle of a pizza. Measured in degrees (0°–360°).
兩條半徑在圓心形成的角——就像扇子的開口角度或披薩的切角。用度數衡量（0°–360°）。

θ = (arc length ÷ circumference) × 360°
🌈

Arc Length 弧長

The distance along the curved edge between two points — like the length of shoreline between two piers. It's a fraction of the full circumference based on the angle.
圓弧上兩點之間的曲線距離——就像兩個碼頭之間的海岸線長度。根據角度，它是整個周長的一部分。

s = (θ ÷ 360) × 2πr
🏖️

Sector Area 扇形面積

The area of the "pizza slice" — the region enclosed by two radii and the arc. It's a fraction of the full circle's area based on the angle.
「披薩片」的面積——由兩條半徑和弧圍成的區域。根據角度，它是整個圓面積的一部分。

Asector = (θ ÷ 360) × πr²
🚧

Sector Perimeter 扇形周界

The total distance around the sector — two straight edges (radii) plus the curved arc. Like fencing around a pie slice.
扇形的總邊長——兩條直邊（半徑）加上弧長。就像在一塊派的四周圍上柵欄。

Psector = 2r + arc length
🪀

Chord 弦

A straight line connecting two points on the circle — like a rope stretched across a bay. The diameter is the longest possible chord.
連接圓上兩點的直線——就像一條繩子橫跨海灣。直徑是最長的弦。

chord = 2r × sin(θ ÷ 2)
