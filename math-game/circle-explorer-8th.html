Radius

The distance from the center to any point on the edge. Every radius in the same circle is equal.

r = d ÷ 2
d

Diameter

A line through the center connecting two edge points. Always twice the radius.

d = 2r
C

Circumference

The distance around the circle. Uses the special number π ≈ 3.14159.

C = 2πr = πd
A

Area

The space inside. Double the radius and the area quadruples!

A = πr²
