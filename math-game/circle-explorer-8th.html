Radius

The radius is the distance from the center of the circle to any point on its edge. Every radius of the same circle is the same length.

r = d ÷ 2

Diameter

The diameter is a straight line passing through the center, connecting two points on the circle. It's always twice the radius.

d = 2r

Circumference

The circumference is the distance around the circle — like wrapping a string around it and measuring. It uses the special number π ≈ 3.14159.

C = 2πr   or   C = πd

Area

The area is the space inside the circle. Think of it as how much paint you'd need to fill it in.

A = πr²

How are they connected?

Everything starts with the radius. Double it to get diameter. Multiply by 2π for circumference. Square it and multiply by π for area. Move the slider above and watch all the values change together!
