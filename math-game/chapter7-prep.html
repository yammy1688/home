📖 7.4 — Area of Composite Figures

A composite figure is built from simple shapes. Two strategies: ADD the pieces, or draw a bigger shape and SUBTRACT what you don’t want.

Key Formulas / 關鍵公式

Rectangle 長方形

A = l × w
length × width

Triangle 三角形

A = ½ × b × h
base × height ÷ 2

Trapezoid 梯形

A = ½(b₁ + b₂) × h
avg of parallel sides × height

Parallelogram 平行四邊形

A = b × h
base × perpendicular height

Circle 圓形

A = πr²
Semicircle = ½πr²

Method 方法

ADD  or  SUBTRACT
加法法 或 減去法
🏛️
Example 1: Trapezoid + Semicircle / 梯形加半圓
ADD METHOD / 加法法
1
Identify the pieces / 找出各部分
A trapezoid (top = 10, bottom = 16, height = 6) with a semicircle of radius 5 sitting on the top edge.
2
Area of the trapezoid / 梯形面積
Atrap = ½(10 + 16) × 6 = ½ × 26 × 6 = 78 cm²
3
Area of the semicircle / 半圓面積 (use π ≈ 3.14)
Asemi = ½ × 3.14 × 5² = ½ × 3.14 × 25 = 39.25 cm²
4
Add them up / 相加
Total = 78 + 39.25 = 117.25 cm²
💡 Pro Tip
When the curve sits on a straight edge, the flat side of the semicircle IS that edge — no overlap to worry about. / 半圓坐在直邊上時，不用擔心重疊。
🏃
Example 2: Running-Track Field / 跑道操場
ADD METHOD / 加法法
1
Break it down / 拆解
One rectangle in the middle + two semicircles (one on each end). Two semicircles = one full circle.
2
Rectangle area / 長方形面積
Arect = 80 × 40 = 3,200 m²
3
Two semicircles = one circle / 兩個半圓 = 一個圓, radius = 20
Acircle = πr² = 3.14 × 20² = 3.14 × 400 = 1,256 m²
4
Total field area / 總面積
Total = 3,200 + 1,256 = 4,456 m²
💡 Pro Tip
Two semicircles of equal radius always fuse into one full circle — saves a multiplication! / 兩個相等半徑的半圓合併為一個圓，省一步。
🏴
Example 3: Flag — Square minus Triangle / 旗子 (正方形減三角形)
SUBTRACT METHOD / 減去法
1
Big shape first / 先找大形狀
The whole flag is a 12×12 square; a right triangle is cut from one corner with legs 5 and 12.
2
Square area / 正方形面積
Asq = 12 × 12 = 144 in²
3
Triangle area / 三角形面積
Atri = ½ × 5 × 12 = 30 in²
4
Subtract / 相減
Flag area = 144 − 30 = 114 in²
💡 Pro Tip
When the leftover shape has a weird angled edge, SUBTRACT is almost always easier than breaking the shape into triangles. / 剩下形狀有斜邊時，減去法通常比拆三角形更容易。
🌸
Example 4: Petal inside a Square / 正方形中的花瓣
INTERSECTION / 相交區域
1
See the trick / 看出巧思
Two quarter-circles (each with r = 14) are drawn from opposite corners of a 14×14 square. The petal is the overlap of both quarter-circles.
2
Area of one quarter-circle / 一個四分之一圓
AQ = ¼ × 3.14 × 14² = ¼ × 615.44 = 153.86 cm²
3
Both quarters together cover the square, PLUS the petal twice / 兩個四分之一覆蓋整個正方形，並且多覆蓋花瓣一次
2 × Q = square + petal
2(153.86) = 196 + petal
petal = 307.72 − 196 = 111.72 cm²
4
Remaining paper (square minus petal) / 剩餘部分
Remaining = 196 − 111.72 = 84.28 cm²
That’s the total area of the two crescent-shaped pieces on the outside. / 外圍兩塊月牙的總面積。
💡 Inclusion-Exclusion
When two shapes overlap, A ∪ B = A + B − (A ∩ B). Solve for the overlap when you know the union. / 知道聯集時，反過來解交集。

📦 7.5 — Volume of Prisms

A solid is a prism if every slice parallel to the base is congruent to the base. That one property is the whole reason the volume formula works.

Key Formulas / 關鍵公式

Prism Volume 柱體體積

V = (base area) × height
The golden rule for every prism

Cross-Section Rule 截面性質

slices ∥ base ⇒ congruent
That’s what defines a prism

Rectangular 長方柱

V = l × w × h
Base is a rectangle

Triangular 三角柱

V = ½ b h × L
L = length of the prism

Trapezoidal 梯形柱

V = ½(b₁+b₂)h × L
Trapezoid base × depth

Any Polygon 任意多邊形

V = (base A) × L
Pentagon, hexagon — same rule
🧊
Example 1: Rectangular Prism / 長方柱
V = l × w × h
1
Given / 已知
length = 8 cm, width = 5 cm, height = 6 cm.
2
Area of base / 底面積
Abase = 8 × 5 = 40 cm²
3
Volume / 體積
V = 40 × 6 = 240 cm³
💡 Pro Tip
Any face can be the “base” — just make sure height is the distance perpendicular to that face. / 任一面可當底，高度必須垂直於底。
🔺
Example 2: Triangular Prism (tent) / 三角柱 (帳篷)
V = ½bh · L
1
Given / 已知
Triangular base: legs 4 cm × 3 cm (right triangle). Prism length L = 10 cm.
2
Base area / 底面積
Abase = ½ × 4 × 3 = 6 cm²
3
Volume / 體積
V = 6 × 10 = 60 cm³
💡 Pro Tip
For a non-right triangle, use the perpendicular height, not a slanted side. / 非直角三角形要用垂直高，不是斜邊。
🛢️
Example 3: Trapezoidal Prism / 梯形柱
V = ½(b₁+b₂)h · L
1
Given / 已知
Trapezoid base: parallel sides 5 in and 8 in, height between them 4 in. Prism length L = 12 in.
2
Base area / 底面積
Abase = ½(5 + 8) × 4 = ½ × 13 × 4 = 26 in²
3
Volume / 體積
V = 26 × 12 = 312 in³
💡 Pro Tip
Don’t mix up the trapezoid’s internal height (between parallel sides) with the prism length. Different axes! / 別把梯形內部的高和柱體的長度混淆！
✂️
Example 4: What’s the Cross-Section? / 截面形狀
CONCEPTUAL / 概念題
1
Slice the prism parallel to its base / 平行於底切開
A hexagonal prism is sliced parallel to its hexagonal base. What shape is the cut?
2
Prism rule / 柱體性質
Every slice parallel to the base of a prism is congruent to the base — same shape, same size.
3
Conclusion / 結論
Cross-section = congruent hexagon
💡 Non-Prism Warning
A cone or pyramid does NOT have this property — parallel slices shrink toward the apex. That’s why the volume formula changes (V = ⅓ base × h). / 錐體的平行截面會縮小，體積公式不同 (V = 底 × 高 ÷ 3)。

🏠 7.6 — Real-World Problems: Surface Area & Volume

Surface area = total area of all the outside faces. The cleanest way to find it: imagine unfolding the solid into a flat net, then add every piece.

Key Formulas / 關鍵公式

Net Method 展開圖

SA = Σ (all faces)
Unfold → add every piece

Rectangular Prism 長方柱

SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)
Three pairs of matching rectangles

Triangular Prism 三角柱

SA = 2 · Atri + (perim)L
2 triangular ends + 3 rectangle sides

Composite Solid 組合體

Vtotal = V₁ + V₂
Split, compute, add

Hidden Face Warning ⚠️

SA ≠ SA₁ + SA₂
Subtract glued faces (counted twice)

Units 單位

Volume: cm³  SA: cm²
Volume = cubed, Area = squared
🧱
Example 1: Wooden Block / 木塊
RECTANGULAR PRISM / 長方柱
1
Given / 已知
l = 5 cm, w = 3 cm, h = 4 cm. Find volume AND surface area.
2
Volume / 體積
V = 5 × 3 × 4 = 60 cm³
3
Surface area from the net / 利用展開圖
Top & bottom: each 5×3 = 15 ⇒ 30
Front & back: each 5×4 = 20 ⇒ 40
Left & right: each 3×4 = 12 ⇒ 24
SA = 30 + 40 + 24 = 94 cm²
4
Check with the formula / 用公式核對
SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2(15 + 20 + 12) = 2(47) = 94 cm² ✓
💡 Pro Tip
Sketching the net always beats memorizing the formula — even works on weird shapes where no formula exists. / 畫展開圖比背公式好，遇到怪形狀也能做。
🐦
Example 2: Birdhouse — Pentagon Prism / 五邊形柱屋
COMPOSITE BASE / 組合底面
1
Given / 已知
The base is a pentagon = rectangle (6 × 4) with a triangle on top (base 6, height 3). Prism length L = 10.
2
Base area (composite) / 底面積
Abase = 6×4 + ½×6×3 = 24 + 9 = 33 cm²
3
Volume / 體積
V = 33 × 10 = 330 cm³
4
Surface area / 表面積
Two pentagon ends: 2 × 33 = 66
Roof slant sides: slant = √(3² + 3²) = √18 ≈ 4.24; two slanted rectangles 2 × 4.24 × 10 ≈ 84.85
Vertical walls (left & right): 2 × 4 × 10 = 80
Bottom: 6 × 10 = 60
SA ≈ 66 + 84.85 + 80 + 60 = 290.85 cm²
💡 Pro Tip
For a prism, the side faces are always rectangles whose widths match each edge of the base — one rectangle per edge. Count edges to count rectangles. / 柱體的側面都是長方形，每個底邊對一個矩形。
📐
Example 3: L-shaped Prism Volume / L形柱體積
SPLIT METHOD / 分割法
1
Given / 已知
An L-shaped base: big rectangle 10 × 6 with a 4 × 3 rectangle removed from the corner. Prism depth L = 5.
2
Base area by subtraction / 用減法求底面積
Abase = 10×6 − 4×3 = 60 − 12 = 48 cm²
3
Volume / 體積
V = 48 × 5 = 240 cm³
4
Check with split method / 用分割法核對
Two rectangles: 10×3 (bottom strip) + 6×3 (side strip) = 30 + 18 = 48. ✓
V = 48 × 5 = 240 cm³
💡 Pro Tip
For any oddly-shaped prism, the game is always: find the base area (add or subtract simple shapes), then multiply by depth. / 奇形柱體：先求底面積，再乘以長度。
